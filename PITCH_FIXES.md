# Fixes needed in the ideathon pitch

Checked against the real data. Fix these before submitting or presenting.

1. **Wrong station code.** The pitch says "Dehri-on-Sone (DDU)". Dehri-on-Sone is **DOS**, 17 km from Sasaram. **DDU** is a different station, Pt. Deen Dayal Upadhyaya Jn (earlier Mughalsarai, `MGS` in the data), about 97 km away. The rename to DDU is from public knowledge, not from the dataset; confirm on IRCTC.
2. **Wrong direction claim.** The pitch says Patna and Gaya move the passenger "toward the destination". For Sasaram to Delhi they are east of Sasaram and Delhi is west. They belong under "nearby stations", not "toward the destination".
3. **Tagline mismatch.** Pitch text says "Seat nahi? Raah hai." The logo files say "Seat nahi? Raah hai na." Use the logo version everywhere.
4. **"Every station within 200 km".** The prototype uses 54 corridor stations, not every station. Say "major stations" or "every major junction".
5. **"Direct trains" examples.** In the 2016 data only 4 trains halt at both Sasaram and New Delhi. Do not say "many". Re-check on the current timetable before quoting a number.
6. **Unproven impact claims.** "Helps Indian Railways use capacity better" and "brings more passengers" are claims. Add a corridor estimate or phrase as a goal.
7. **Data source.** "TIES scheme" is stated as the plan. Confirm with the organizers that it applies; otherwise say "approved data partner".
8. **Competition.** Apps such as ixigo, ConfirmTkt and RailYatri exist. State what Raah does differently only after checking their features.
9. **Example route in voice notes.** Muzaffarpur is not between Sasaram and Delhi (north Bihar). Better junction examples: Mirzapur, Varanasi, Prayagraj (Allahabad in the data), Kanpur.
